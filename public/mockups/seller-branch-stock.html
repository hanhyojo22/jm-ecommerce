Before · Current Live Page

What made the current Branch Stock page hard to use

  • Zero visual stock status: Medicines with 0 on hand look identical to medicines with 220 on hand — staff cannot spot stockouts.
  • Wasted "Option: —" column: 70% of the table width is blank whitespace with an em-dash because most medicines don't have color/size variants.
  • No status tabs or filters: No way to filter to Out of stock, Low stock, or Prescription (Rx) items during replenishment.
  • Easy to lose edits: No indication of which inputs were changed, what the previous count was, or a sticky save bar until scrolling past 25 rows.
  • No company-wide context for Head Office: Head office cannot see how a branch's stock compares to the rest of the pharmacy chain.
After · Impeccable Seller Center Design

How the redesigned workspace solves every friction point

  • Clickable 4-metric health strip & status tabs: One click filters to Out of stock (5), Low stock (3), Rx, or Unsaved edits.
  • Rich pharmacy catalog rows: Dosage-form icons, Generic Name + Strength, SKU, Rx/OTC badges, unit price, and inline variant pills only when options exist.
  • Tactile steppers + Quick-add chips: Touch-friendly − / + buttons plus +10, +50, and 0 pills for fast delivery receiving.
  • Live delta tracking & floating save capsule: Edited rows highlight in blue with Was 89 · +31 and row-level Undo, plus Seller Center's .ps-savebar capsule.
  • Rapid Shelf-Count & Multi-Branch Matrix: Keyboard Enter/↓ cycle-counting mode for branch staff, and a 3-branch matrix view for Head Office.
