What this mockup changes

  • Store bar: store name stays the one bold thing; order number and total sit on a quiet 12px meta line beneath it.
  • State: the filled pill becomes a 6px dot plus a 12px label, so the bar no longer reads as a row of buttons.
  • Chat: “Chat with seller” becomes an icon-only ghost button with a tooltip and accessible label.
  • Items: 56px thumbnails, a fixed two-line name area that breaks after the sixth word on wide screens (phones keep natural wrapping), a quieter variant line, right-aligned unit price, and ×2 instead of “Qty: 2”.
  • Long orders: only the first two items are listed; “+ 1 more item” opens the order for the rest.
  • Total: moves into the header meta line, so no footer is needed.

Unchanged: day groups and their running totals, status wording and colors, two-row product names, and row links to the order page.
