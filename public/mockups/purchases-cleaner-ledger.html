What Variant B changes

  • One sheet per day: orders share a single bordered surface and a hairline divider, instead of each carrying its own border and shadow.
  • Total up top: the order total sits at the top right, where the eye already lands, and the status moves into the quiet meta line with the order number.
  • No per-item price: rows keep only the photo, two-row name, variant, and quantity, so names get noticeably more width.
  • Six-word break: on wide screens, product names break after the sixth word so long names start with a balanced two-line rhythm before the clamp. Phones keep natural wrapping to avoid clipping words.
  • 44px photos: smaller than Variant A, which tightens each row and lets more of the list fit on a phone.
  • Chat: still one icon-only ghost button beside the total.

Trade-off: unit prices are no longer visible in the list — buyers see them on the order page. Variant A keeps the per-item price and separate cards if that matters more.
