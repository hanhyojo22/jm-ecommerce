UI/UX Design Architecture & System

Mobile Homepage Experience

This high-fidelity mockup visualizes the primary mobile buyer experience for JMMart. It bridges the existing Laravel backend (multi-vendor catalog, stores, categories) with the Android/iOS Flutter app (CatalogScreen, Riverpod state, and AppShell navigation).

Color System Tokens
  • Brand Teal: #14665A (Primary Header)
  • Warm Amber: #E0A63B (Accent & CTAs)
  • Light Surface: #F7F6F2 (Warm Sand)
  • Dark Surface: #0F1513 (OLED Dark)
Flutter & Riverpod Seams
  • Slivers Hierarchy: CustomScrollView
  • State Providers: catalogFilterProvider
  • Feed Lazy Loading: productsProvider.loadMore()
  • Shell Sync: shellTabProvider (Tab 0)

Strategic UX Modules

1
Unified Search & Delivery Location Header: Combines one-tap address selection with an unconstrained 48dp search pill that accommodates both direct queries, photo lookup, and quick category filtering.
2
Multi-Vendor Storefront Spotlight: Highlights independent merchants (e.g. Verde Botanicals, Apex Tech) with verified merchant badges, direct follower counts, and ratings to build buyer trust.
3
Urgency & Conversion Trigger (Flash Deals): High-contrast dark card container with active countdown timer and stock claim percentage bar to drive impulsive discovery without cluttering the clean catalog aesthetic.
4
2-Column Native Masonry Product Cards: Optimized for thumb accessibility on 375px–430px screens. Features explicit seller attribution, ratings count, clear PHP currency formatting, and one-tap "+ Add" cart actions.
Accessibility & Touch Target Compliance: Every tap target satisfies Android Material 3 and Apple HIG requirements (≥ 44×44dp). Text contrast exceeds WCAG AA (4.5:1), and color is never the sole carrier of status.
