# Everyday Pharmacy

An online pharmacy where customers order medicines and everyday goods from a nearby pharmacy branch, through the mobile app.

## Language

**Branch**:
One physical pharmacy location that sells and fulfils orders.
_Avoid_: Store, shop, outlet

**Cart**:
The products a customer is about to order, all from a single Branch.
_Avoid_: Basket, bag

**Cart item**:
One product (or one variant of a product) in a Cart, with how many of it the customer wants.
_Avoid_: Cart line, line, entry
