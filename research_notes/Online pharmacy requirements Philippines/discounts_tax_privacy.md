# Philippine discount, tax, receipt and data privacy rules for a multi-branch online pharmacy

Research date: 2026-11-15. Most sources are secondary: news, law firms and accounting firms. Several primary PDFs (DTI JAO, DOH and FDA circulars) could not be fetched (403 or scanned image). Each item says where it came from. Verify against the primary text before relying on anything for compliance.

## Senior citizen discount (RA 9994 amending RA 7432): 20% + VAT exemption on medicines, online/delivery, proof, booklet, computation, delivery fees, records

### Takeaway
Seniors get a 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicines. The seller removes the 12% VAT first (price / 1.12), then takes 20% off that VAT-exclusive base. Since DOH AO 2024-0017 (23 Dec 2024) and FDA Circular 2025-005 (4 Sep 2025), seniors no longer need the purchase booklet for medicines. They still need an OSCA/senior ID and a doctor's prescription. Online and delivery orders qualify: the customer declares senior status before ordering and shows the ID on delivery. The seller must keep a separate sales record for these sales.

### Cited Findings
- DOH Administrative Order 2024-0017, signed 23 Dec 2024, removed the purchase booklet requirement from the DOH implementing guidelines under the Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010. A valid ID and a doctor's prescription are still required. — [Philstar, 23 Dec 2024](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2024/12/23/2409368/one-less-hassle-senior-citizens-booklet-rule-medicines-scrapped); [Philstar, 24 Dec 2024](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2024/12/24/2409468/booklets-meds-no-longer-required-seniors-doh)
- FDA Circular No. 2025-005 (4 Sep 2025) formally removed the booklet from the senior checklist for the 20% discount and 12% VAT exemption on medicines and medical devices. The FDA said the law itself never required a booklet; only earlier DOH orders did. — [Manila Bulletin, 4 Sep 2025](https://mb.com.ph/2025/09/04/fda-scraps-senior-citizen-purchase-booklet-for-medicine-device-discounts) (only the search snippet was read; the page returned 403); [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/manila/fda-seniors-no-longer-need-purchase-booklets-for-meds)
- Computation under BIR RR 7-2010 as clarified by RMC 38-2012: Total billing amount / 1.12 (removes 12% VAT) x 20% = discount. The VAT-removal step applies only to VAT-registered sellers. — [Davao City OSCA copy of RMC 38-2012](https://seniorcitizen.davaocity.gov.ph/index.php/republic-act-9994-page-4); [Kassly POS guide](https://kasslypos.com/guides/senior-citizen-pwd-discount-computation)
  - Worked example (inference from the formula): a medicine priced at PHP 112.00 VAT-inclusive becomes 100.00 VAT-exempt, minus 20.00 discount, so the customer pays 80.00.
- What the invoice/receipt must show (RR 7-2010): the gross selling price and the discount as separate lines; the words "VAT Exempt Sale" printed prominently; VAT-exempt sales kept apart from taxable sales; VAT on any taxable items in the same order billed separately. One sample invoice shows the "VAT Exempt Sale" amount net of the discount, but sources differ on layout. — [Davao City OSCA / RMC 38-2012](https://seniorcitizen.davaocity.gov.ph/index.php/republic-act-9994-page-4); [jur.ph summary of RR 7-2010](https://jur.ph/law/summary/implementing-tax-privileges-republic-act-9994-senior-citizens)
- Records: the seller must keep a separate, accurate record of these sales with the senior's name, OSCA ID number, gross sales, discount granted, transaction date and invoice number. Input VAT on the exempt sale cannot be claimed as a credit and goes to cost/expense. — [jur.ph RR 7-2010](https://jur.ph/law/summary/implementing-tax-privileges-republic-act-9994-senior-citizens); [Davao City OSCA](https://seniorcitizen.davaocity.gov.ph/index.php/republic-act-9994-page-4)
- Tax treatment of the discount: under RA 9994 the establishment may deduct the discount from gross income as a deduction (the old tax credit no longer applies). — [Philstar on BIR RR 7-2010, 22 Jul 2010](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2010/07/22/595250/bir-issues-implementing-rules-expanded-senior-citizens-act/amp/)
- Online/phone/SMS orders: a DTI-led Joint Memorandum Circular (2022) applies senior/PWD discounts to online and phone orders. The customer declares senior/PWD status to the platform or merchant before placing the order and presents the same proof when the goods are delivered. — [ABS-CBN, 2 Aug 2022](https://www.abs-cbn.com/business/08/02/22/dti-extends-senior-and-pwd-discounts-to-online-buys); [GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/832373/senior-citizens-pwds-now-entitled-to-discounts-in-online-transactions/story); search-result excerpt of DTI guidelines via [Philstar 2022](https://www.philstar.com/business/2022/06/10/2187242/senior-pwd-discounts-pushed-online-purchases)
- A BIR issuance (May 2022) confirmed the senior/PWD discount covers SMS and online purchases. — [Manila Bulletin, 20 May 2022](https://mb.com.ph/2022/05/20/senior-citizens-pwd-price-discount-covers-sms-online-purchases-bir) (headline/snippet only; the page returned 403, so the issuance number was not confirmed)
- Under the DTI online guidelines, medicine purchases are not subject to the PHP 1,300/week cap that applies to basic necessities and prime commodities. — search-result summary of the [DTI-DA-DOE Joint Administrative Order 2024 PDF](https://dtiwebfiles.s3.ap-southeast-1.amazonaws.com/Laws+and+Policies/Consumer+Laws/EX00326600+Joint+Administrative+Order+-+2024+Revised+Rules+on+Granting+Special+Discount+to+Senior+Citizens+&+PWDs+on+Purchase+of+BNPCs.pdf) and [Manila Standard](https://manilastandard.net/?p=314249044) (the PDF is a scanned image and could not be read directly)
- Delivery fees: a 2021 report quoted advocates saying delivery charges are not included in the 20% discount. — [Philstar, 31 May 2021](https://philstar.com/headlines/2021/05/31/2102109/rules-online-discounts-pwds-seniors-still-pending-months-after-request) (secondary source; not confirmed in the regulation text)
- Discount sharing: DOH guidelines (AO 2010-0032, under RA 9994) address how the 20% medicine discount is shared between manufacturers/distributors and retailers. — [jur.ph summary](https://jur.ph/law/summary/guidelines-and-mechanisms-to-implement-the-provisions-of-ra-9994-re-sale-of-medicines-and-the-sharing-of-the-20-senior-citizens-discount); [Philstar 2011](https://www.philstar.com/business/2011/02/22/659273/manufacturerdistributors-and-wholesaler-retailers-share-senior-citizen-discount-whats-this)

### Inferences
- Checkout flow: (1) the customer marks themselves as a senior at checkout and enters their OSCA ID number, possibly uploading a photo of the ID; (2) the medicine lines need a valid prescription, which the Rx upload flow can supply; (3) the rider or pickup staff checks the physical ID against the name on the order before handing it over; (4) if the check fails, the order goes back to the full price. This follows the DTI "declare before ordering, show proof on delivery" model.
- Pricing engine: store VAT-inclusive prices. For discounted lines, compute `vat_exempt_base = price / 1.12` and `discount = 0.20 * vat_exempt_base`, round to the centavo, and keep gross, VAT-exempt base, discount and net per line. Non-medicine items (e.g., cosmetics) in the same cart are NOT discounted under the medicine rule and stay VATable. That means each product needs a "medicine/eligible" flag.
- Delivery fee: safest default is no discount on the delivery fee, flagged as uncertain.
- The discount does not stack with the PWD discount (RA 9994/RA 10754 let a person claim only one), but this is from background knowledge and not sourced in this session. See Gaps.
- Reporting: a "Senior/PWD sales book" report per branch (name, ID number, date, invoice number, gross, discount) is required.

### Gaps
- Could not read the FDA Circular 2025-005 text. Not confirmed: whether it requires the drug outlet to annotate or keep the prescription, whether a quantity limit (e.g., a one-month supply) applies, or how it treats online/delivery orders.
- Could not confirm the exact DTI JMC number for online discounts (reported as Joint Memorandum Circular No. 01, 2022) or the BIR 2022 issuance number.
- No authoritative source found on whether a delivery fee charged by the pharmacy itself (not a third-party courier) is discountable.
- Not confirmed: the rule that a person cannot combine the senior and PWD discounts, or combine them with promo discounts (customer picks the higher one). This is commonly cited but no source was retrieved.

## PWD discount (RA 9442 / RA 10754): ID, booklet, computation

### Takeaway
RA 10754 (2016) gives PWDs the same 20% discount plus VAT exemption on medicines, computed the same way as for seniors. Unlike seniors, PWDs still appear to need the purchase booklet for medicines: DOH AO 2017-0008 requires it, and no issuance removing it was found as of the latest sources (Sep 2025).

### Cited Findings
- DOH AO 2017-0008 requires PWDs to present a purchase booklet for the medicine discount. Advocates asked DOH in Dec 2024 to extend the booklet removal to PWDs. — [Philstar, 30 Dec 2024](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2024/12/30/2410678/remove-medicine-booklet-requirement-pwds-too)
- January 2025: the FDA said no administrative order had yet been issued to bring PWDs under the booklet-free rule, and that it was studying an expansion. — [Philstar, 7 Jan 2025](https://philstar.com/bansa/2025/01/07/2412521/fda-studies-extending-booklet-free-medicine-discount-pwds); [Philstar Cebu, 8 Jan 2025](https://philstar.com/cebu-news/2025/01/08/2412679/booklet-free-medicine-discount-pwds-eyed); [Edge Davao](https://edgedavao.net/the-economy/2025/01/fda-eyes-expanded-discount-on-meds-for-elderly-pwds)
- FDA Circular 2025-005 (Sep 2025) covers seniors, not PWDs. — search-result summaries of [Manila Bulletin, 4 Sep 2025](https://mb.com.ph/2025/09/04/fda-scraps-senior-citizen-purchase-booklet-for-medicine-device-discounts)
- The same computation (price / 1.12 x 20%) and the same POS treatment apply to senior and PWD sales. — [Kassly POS guide](https://kasslypos.com/guides/senior-citizen-pwd-discount-computation)
- The DTI online-discount guidelines cover both seniors and PWDs: declare before ordering, present the ID (and booklet where required) on delivery. — [ABS-CBN 2022](https://www.abs-cbn.com/business/08/02/22/dti-extends-senior-and-pwd-discounts-to-online-buys); [Philstar 2022 (PWD advocates)](https://philstar.com/headlines/2022/05/31/2185041/pwd-advocates-urge-expansion-online-discount-guidelines-delivery-platforms)

### Inferences
- Build the discount with a type setting (`senior` | `pwd`) whose proof rules can be configured: senior needs ID + Rx; PWD needs ID + Rx + booklet (for now). That way, a later DOH/FDA change for PWDs is a config change, not new code.
- For online orders, the booklet cannot be stamped remotely. A practical approach is to require the booklet at handover, with the rider or pickup counter recording or stamping it. This is an operational inference; no DOH guidance on remote booklet entries was found.
- Record keeping is the same as for seniors: name, PWD ID number, date, invoice number, gross, discount. The PWD ID number is also health-related personal data (see the privacy section).

### Gaps
- No source dated after Sep 2025 checked whether DOH/FDA has since removed the PWD booklet. Re-check doh.gov.ph and fda.gov.ph before go-live.
- The BIR regulation for PWD discounts (commonly cited as RR 5-2017 under RA 10754) was not retrieved or verified.

## Other mandated discounts and medicine VAT rules (solo parents, VAT-exempt maintenance drugs list)

### Takeaway
(1) Under RA 11861 (Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act), qualifying solo parents earning under PHP 250,000 a year get 10% off plus VAT exemption on medicines, vaccines, milk, food, supplements and diapers for children up to age 6. They show a Solo Parent ID and booklet. BIR RR 1-2023 implements it. (2) Under TRAIN/CREATE, medicines for diabetes, high cholesterol, hypertension, cancer, mental illness, tuberculosis and kidney disease are VAT-exempt for everyone if they appear on the FDA-endorsed list that the BIR publishes through RMCs. As of August 2026 the list has 2,277 drugs, and it is updated quarterly (Feb/May/Aug/Nov).

### Cited Findings
- Solo parents earning less than PHP 250,000 a year get a 10% discount and VAT exemption on their child's milk, food, micronutrient supplements and sanitary diapers. — [DSWD](https://www.dswd.gov.ph/?p=83983)
- BIR RR 1-2023 lists the covered items: milk, diapers, medicines, vaccines and other medical supplements, including purchases from drug stores and pharmacies. Proof is the Solo Parent Identification Card (SPIC) plus the Solo Parent Booklet, and sellers may not bill VAT on qualifying sales. — [KPMG on RR 1-2023](https://kpmg.com/ph/en/home/insights/2023/03/naytay.html); [Philstar, 7 Mar 2023](https://www.philstar.com/business/2023/03/07/2249742/naytay); [Scribd copy of RR 1-2023](https://www.scribd.com/document/642985915/RR-No-1-2023)
- The benefit covers children up to 6 years old. — [Rappler](https://www.rappler.com/nation/benefits-families-get-expanded-solo-parents-law/)
- The IRR was signed in Sept 2022 (DSWD says 20 Sep; CHR says 30 Sep). — [GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/846404/irr-for-law-giving-added-benefits-to-single-parents-signed/story); [CHR](https://chr.gov.ph/statement-of-the-commission-on-human-rights-lauding-the-signing-of-the-irr-for-expanded-solo-parents-act/)
- VAT-exempt medicines list: the exemption covers medicines for diabetes, high cholesterol, hypertension, cancer, mental illness, tuberculosis and kidney disease (dialysis). The FDA recommends medicines, and the BIR publishes them through Revenue Memorandum Circulars. — [DOF](https://www.dof.gov.ph/more-medicines-for-cancer-diabetes-dialysis-hypertension-high-cholesterol-and-mental-illnesses-are-now-vat-free/); [Grant Thornton on RMC 108-2025](https://grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/rmc-108-2025-updated-vat-exempt-medicines-for-broader-healthcare-access)
- August 2026: the BIR added 14 drugs, bringing the list to 2,277 (cancer 708, hypertension 537, diabetes 331, mental illness 300, high cholesterol 172, kidney disease 152, TB 77). The legal basis is TRAIN and CREATE. — [Philstar, 6 Aug 2026](https://www.philstar.com/nation/2026/08/06/2547262/bir-14-more-drugs-exempt-vat) (the article does not give the RMC number)
- Update cadence: the FDA publishes an updated list in February, May, August and November each year. — search-result summary of [GMA / Inquirer coverage](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2078287); see also [FDA Advisory 2025-0510 coverage, Tribune Jun 2025](https://tribune.net.ph/2025/06/14/more-meds-for-diabetes-hypertension-exempted-from-vat)
- 2025 examples: March 2025 added 12 cholesterol, 3 diabetes, 1 hypertension and 1 mental-illness drug. June 2025 (FDA Advisory 2025-0510) added 10 more. — [GMA](https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/money/economy/941898/more-medicines-for-diabetes-hypertension-high-cholesterol-made-vat-free/story); [Tribune](https://tribune.net.ph/2025/06/14/more-meds-for-diabetes-hypertension-exempted-from-vat)

### Inferences
- Each product (SKU) needs a `vat_status` flag (VATable / VAT-exempt-listed), keyed by the FDA registration number and generic name/strength/form as listed. Head office updates it each quarter when the FDA/BIR publishes. For a listed product, the shelf price has no VAT. So when a senior/PWD buys it, the 20% applies to the price as-is (no /1.12 step, because there is no VAT in it). This inference follows from "VAT removed first"; verify with an accountant.
- Solo parent: a third discount type (10%, VAT-exempt), allowed only for items flagged as eligible for children (medicines/vaccines/milk/diapers). It needs the SPIC + booklet and the child's age (under 6). Lower priority for an MVP but legally mandated.
- The VAT-exempt list applies to all buyers, not just seniors, and must show on invoices as a VAT-exempt sale line.

### Gaps
- The latest RMC number (Aug 2026) and any November 2026 update were not found. Check bir.gov.ph's RMC list and the FDA's "VAT-exempt" advisories.
- Not verified: whether RR 1-2023 sets a weekly/monthly cap on solo-parent purchases, and the exact process for children's medicines (whether a prescription in the child's name is needed).
- No other national mandated medicine discount was found in this session (e.g., for indigents or national athletes, RA 10699 gives 20% on medicines). These were not researched.

## BIR invoicing for e-commerce (EOPT Act RA 11976, RR 7-2024/11-2024, EIS)

### Takeaway
Since 27 Apr 2024 (RR 7-2024 under the Ease of Paying Taxes Act RA 11976), the **invoice** is the main proof of sale for both goods and services. Official receipts are now only supplementary documents. VAT-registered sellers must issue a VAT invoice for every sale regardless of amount. E-commerce/internet sellers (except micro taxpayers) must issue electronic invoices through the BIR Electronic Invoicing System (EIS) by **31 Dec 2026** (RR 11-2025 as amended by RR 26-2025; guidelines in RMC 98-2026, 22 Sep 2026).

### Cited Findings
- RA 11976 took effect 22 Jan 2024. RR 7-2024 was published 12 Apr 2024 and its invoicing rules took effect 27 Apr 2024. RR 11-2024 amended it, and RMC 77-2024 (11 Jul 2024) clarified it. — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/clarification-on-the-invoicing-requirements-per-rr-no-7-2024-as-amended-by-rr-no-11-2024/)
- The invoice is now the main record for sales of goods, services or both. Leftover official receipts can be converted by striking out "Official Receipt" and stamping "Invoice". RR 11-2024 removed the deadline for using them up. Official receipts used as supplementary documents must be stamped "This document is not valid for claim of input tax." — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/clarification-on-the-invoicing-requirements-per-rr-no-7-2024-as-amended-by-rr-no-11-2024/); [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/taxwise-or-otherwise/2024/compliance-change-on-invoicing-under-eopt.html); [KPMG](https://kpmg.com/ph/en/home/insights/2024/07/compliance-an-update-to-registration-procedures-and-invoicing-requirements.html)
- VAT-registered sellers must issue a VAT invoice for every sale regardless of amount. Non-VAT sellers must issue one when a sale exceeds PHP 500, when the buyer asks, or when small sales add up to more than PHP 500. The threshold is adjusted for inflation every 3 years from 22 Jan 2024. — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/clarification-on-the-invoicing-requirements-per-rr-no-7-2024-as-amended-by-rr-no-11-2024/)
- For a VAT-registered buyer to claim input VAT, the invoice must show the sales amount, the VAT amount, the registered name and TIN of both buyer and seller, a description of goods, and the date. Different invoice types need separate serial number series. Switching from receipts to invoices needs a new Authority To Print (ATP), and POS/CAS systems needed an Annex D notice. — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/insights/articles-and-updates1/tax-notes/clarification-on-the-invoicing-requirements-per-rr-no-7-2024-as-amended-by-rr-no-11-2024/)
- E-invoicing: RR 11-2025 originally set a 14 Mar 2026 deadline. RR 26-2025 (5 Sep 2025) moved it to 31 Dec 2026. Phase 1 covers e-commerce/internet-transaction taxpayers (micro taxpayers excluded, though they may join voluntarily), large taxpayers, and CAS/CBA users. The deadline covers e-invoice issuance and transmission to the EIS; the Electronic Sales Reporting System (ESRS) is under separate rules. RMC 98-2026 (22 Sep 2026) gives the implementing guidelines. — [Sovos](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/vat/philippines-deadline-for-mandatory-structured-e-invoicing-extended/); [Cygnet](https://www.cygnet.one/ph/blog/rr-11-2025-rr-26-2025-and-rmc-98-2026/); [Daily Tribune, 23 Sep 2026](https://tribune.net.ph/2026/09/23/bir-sets-31-december-e-invoicing-deadline); [Fiscal Requirements](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/5954-philippines-e-invoicing-which-businesses-must-comply-by-december-31-2026)
- RA 11967 Sec. 23(h): e-retailers must issue paper or electronic invoices/receipts for all sales. — [RA 11967, lawphil](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html)
- E-marketplaces must collect the merchant's BIR Certificate of Registration. — [SunStar on ITA enforcement](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/dti-begins-full-enforcement-of-e-commerce-law-liability-rules-2)

### Inferences
- Multi-branch: each branch is usually a separately registered BIR branch (its own branch code and its own invoice serial series/ATP or CAS permit). The order record should carry the fulfilling branch, and the invoice should be issued under that branch's series. This follows standard BIR registration practice; branch-level rules were not checked in this session.
- An invoice usually needs these fields: seller registered name, "VAT Reg. TIN" plus branch code, business address, "Invoice" title, serial number, date, buyer name/address/TIN (TIN mainly for B2B or input-tax claims), item description, qty, unit price, amount, and VATable / VAT-exempt / zero-rated / VAT amount breakdown. Senior/PWD orders add the discount line and the customer's ID number and signature. The full field list is commonly cited from RR 7-2024 Sec. 6 but was not retrieved here.
- COD: invoice when the sale happens. Whether that means order confirmation or delivery/payment is an accounting decision; the app should be able to issue on delivery and cancel or void unpaid ones.
- The e-invoice system must be built (or a BIR-accredited e-invoicing provider integrated) and transmit to the EIS by 31 Dec 2026, unless the company qualifies as a micro taxpayer (gross sales under PHP 3M). A chain is likely above that.
- Selling online also requires DTI E-Commerce Bureau registration, and the BIR CoR should be displayed (see consumer section).

### Gaps
- The complete RR 7-2024 list of required invoice fields, and the e-invoice data format (JSON schema, API certification) under RMC 98-2026, were not retrieved.
- Not confirmed whether the e-invoice deadline has moved again after Sep 2026.
- The receipt fields specific to senior/PWD sales (customer signature and ID number on the invoice) come from older RR 7-2010 practice; whether e-invoices handle this differently is unconfirmed.

## Data Privacy Act (RA 10173) and NPC issuances: prescriptions, consent, retention, breach notification, DPO registration, privacy notice, storage and access

### Takeaway
Health information, including prescriptions and anything showing a customer's conditions such as purchase history of medicines on the VAT-exempt disease list, is **sensitive personal information** under the DPA. Processing it needs a stricter lawful basis (usually consent or a legal-obligation/medical-treatment basis), a privacy impact assessment, and strong security. NPC Circular 16-03 requires notifying the NPC and affected customers within **72 hours** of a breach of sensitive data. NPC Circular 2022-04 requires registering the DPO and data processing systems within 20 days of starting operations, renewed yearly. RA 11967 also obliges e-retailers to follow DPA and NPC security standards.

### Cited Findings
- The DPA treats medical and health information as sensitive personal information. The NPC recommends a privacy impact assessment (PIA) for this kind of processing (see NPC Advisory 2017-03 on PIA). — [NPC Advisory Opinion 2019-032 (MedCheck), Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/702330174/AdOps-2019-032)
- Breach notification (NPC Circular 16-03): notify the NPC within 72 hours of learning of the breach, and notify affected data subjects within 72 hours of reasonable belief that one occurred. This applies when sensitive personal information (or information that could enable identity fraud) is involved and the breach is likely to cause real risk of serious harm. The full report is due within 5 days unless the NPC extends it. — [NDV Law](https://ndvlaw.com/when-must-philippine-companies-report-data-breaches/); [NPC breach slides](https://www.privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/DPO17Breach.pdf); [InCorp](https://philippines.incorp.asia/guides/operationalizing-npc-compliance-and-its-timelines/)
- Breaches are filed through the NPC's Data Breach Notification Management System (DBNMS). NPC Advisory 2026-02 (May 2026) clarifies how to submit through the DBNMS; its text was not reviewed. — [Mondaq on NPC platforms](https://www.mondaq.com/privacy-protection/1319396/npc-rolls-out-breach-notification-and-registration-platforms); search summary
- Registration (NPC Circular 2022-04): DPO and data processing systems are registered together, within 20 days of starting the system or appointing the DPO. The certificate/seal is valid for 1 year, so it must be renewed yearly. — [InCorp](https://philippines.incorp.asia/guides/operationalizing-npc-compliance-and-its-timelines/); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/privacy-protection/1319396/npc-rolls-out-breach-notification-and-registration-platforms)
- RA 11967 Sec. 23(f)(2): e-retailers and online merchants must protect consumer data under RA 10173, follow minimum security standards set by the E-Commerce Bureau and the NPC, and are covered by NPC issuances. — [RA 11967, lawphil](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html)
- The NPC co-signed the ITA IRR (JAO 24-03). — [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/dti-begins-full-enforcement-of-e-commerce-law-liability-rules-2)
- Comparative only (not PH law): EU/UK pharmacy regulators have fined online pharmacies for sending customer data to ad trackers (Meta Pixel), and Estonia's regulator ordered e-pharmacies to stop showing one person's prescriptions to another account holder. — [Usercentrics report](https://usercentrics.com/us/press/pharma-data-compliance-report/?output_format=md); [Pharmaceutical Journal](https://pharmaceutical-journal.com/article/ld/how-pharmacists-can-comply-with-gdpr)

### Inferences
- Treat prescription images, Rx metadata, the patient's condition, senior/PWD ID images and numbers (a PWD ID indicates a disability), and order history of prescription items as sensitive data:
  - Store prescription images in private object storage (not public URLs), serve them only through short-lived signed URLs, and encrypt them at rest.
  - Role-based access: only pharmacists or authorized staff at the fulfilling branch (plus head office compliance) can view Rx images. Log every view (audit trail).
  - No ad/analytics trackers on Rx, order-detail or checkout pages, and no medicine names in push notification text or tracking events.
  - Get explicit, separate, opt-in consent at Rx upload and at senior/PWD ID upload. Do not bundle it with marketing consent. Show a privacy notice in the app: purpose, data collected, retention period, recipients (branch, rider, payment provider), customer rights, and DPO contact.
  - Retention: keep data only as long as the purpose and legal retention periods require. Tax records under the NIRC/EOPT are generally kept 5 years; pharmacy prescription records follow FDA/pharmacy rules (not verified). Then delete or anonymize. Let customers delete their Rx images after the order is done, unless the law requires keeping them.
  - Riders should see only what delivery needs (name, address, phone, payment amount), never the prescription.
  - Incident response: a runbook with a 72-hour clock to the NPC and customers, plus a breach register.
  - Register the DPO and data processing systems with the NPC, and redo the registration when processing changes significantly (new app/Rx system).
- Mobile apps: the in-app privacy notice and consent screens must be shown before collection. Customers must be able to access, correct and delete their data (DPA data subject rights) through account settings or a DPO email.

## Consumer protection for online sales (Internet Transactions Act RA 11967 and IRR JAO 24-03; DTI; Consumer Act on medicine returns)

### Takeaway
RA 11967 (effective Dec 2023, IRR JAO 24-03 signed 24 May 2024, fully enforced from 20 Jun 2025 when the 18-month transition ended) requires an e-retailer to publish on its homepage its corporate/trade name, physical address, phone and email. It must register with the DTI E-Commerce Bureau (Online Business Database), show product name, brand, price, description and condition, issue invoices for all sales, run an internal complaint mechanism, and protect data under the DPA. A complaint left unresolved for 7 calendar days lets the consumer go to DTI. Refunds follow the Consumer Act (RA 7394) remedies. A medicine-specific "no return" rule was not confirmed in sources.

### Cited Findings
- Sec. 23(f)(1): e-retailers must publish on their homepage their corporate and trade names, physical address, contact details (mobile/landline and valid email), and any professional body memberships. These go to the E-Commerce Bureau with at least one government ID or registration document. — [RA 11967, lawphil](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html)
- Sec. 21(g)/22(c)/23(a): offers must show name, brand, price, description and condition. Prices must be shown consistent with Art. 81 of the Consumer Act. — [RA 11967](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html)
- Sec. 23(b)-(c): goods must match their description and be fit for purpose, with labeling in Filipino and/or English. Sec. 23(h): paper or electronic invoices/receipts for all sales. — [RA 11967](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html)
- Sec. 23(i) and Sec. 24: e-retailers must keep an accessible, efficient redress mechanism. Consumers must use it first, and it counts as exhausted after 7 calendar days without resolution. — [RA 11967](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html)
- DTI powers: subpoena (Sec. 13), compliance orders (Sec. 14), takedown orders with a 48-hour hearing window lasting up to 30 days (Sec. 15), and blacklisting (Sec. 16). The merchant is primarily liable to consumers (Sec. 25). — [RA 11967](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html)
- Penalties (Sec. 29): deceptive/unfair practices PHP 20k-100k (1st offense), 100k-500k (2nd), 500k-1M (3rd+). Breaches of the disclosure/redress/data duties PHP 5k-100k (the statute text has drafting errors in the tiers). — [RA 11967](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2023/ra_11967_2023.html)
- IRR = Joint Administrative Order 24-03 (DTI, DICT, DA, DOH, BSP, NPC). Full enforcement began 20 Jun 2025 after the transition ended. Online merchants must submit their information to the E-Commerce Bureau. — [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/dti-begins-full-enforcement-of-e-commerce-law-liability-rules-2); [DTI](https://dti.gov.ph/news/dti-leads-interagency-signing-internet-transactions-act-guidelines); [Baker McKenzie](https://insightplus.bakermckenzie.com/bm/consumer-goods-retail_1/philippines-implementing-rules-of-the-internet-transactions-act-issued)
- IRR signed 24 May 2024; Act effective 20 Dec 2023. — [PwC](https://pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/taxwise-or-otherwise/2024/checking-out-the-Internet-transactions-act.html)
- Remedies for defective or mis-described goods bought online include repair, replacement, refund or price reduction, depending on the defect and the contract. E-retailers should keep a clear cancellation policy. — [NDV Law on refunds](https://ndvlaw.com/how-do-online-buyers-get-refunds-and-returns/); [NDV Law on e-retailer rules](https://ndvlaw.com/regulations-affecting-e-marketplaces-online-or-digital-retailers-and-stores-under-the-internet-transactions-act-of-2023/)
- DTI-DOH-DENR-NPC JAO 22-01 (2022) reportedly requires online businesses to honor Consumer Act refund rights and to show price and a visible refund/return policy. — [lawyer-philippines.com](https://www.lawyer-philippines.com/articles/refund-policies-and-laws-for-online-shopping-platforms) (low-reliability source; verify)
- DTI E-Commerce Bureau contact: ecommerce@dti.gov.ph, (+632) 7791-3282. — [SunStar](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/dti-begins-full-enforcement-of-e-commerce-law-liability-rules-2)

### Inferences
- App/web footer and "About" screen: registered corporate name, trade name, DTI/SEC registration, BIR TIN/CoR, the head office address, and each branch's address, phone and email. Each branch's FDA License to Operate (LTO) should also be shown; that requirement belongs to the FDA/online-pharmacy research track, not this one.
- Product pages: price (VAT-inclusive or VAT-exempt shown clearly), brand, generic name, dosage form, strength, condition.
- A complaints/help-desk module with ticket timestamps and a 7-day resolution target, escalation, and a visible link to the DTI channel.
- Returns policy: dispensed medicines are usually non-returnable for safety reasons, except when the item is wrong, damaged, expired or defective. Those cases should get replacement or refund under the Consumer Act. This is an inference and needs confirmation with FDA/DOH dispensing rules.
